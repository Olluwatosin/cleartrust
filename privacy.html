2. What this policy covers

ClearTrust provides primary source verification and background screening. In doing so we handle two kinds of people's data: our clients (the organizations and individuals who request checks) and subjects (the people a check is about). This policy applies to both.

3. The personal data we collect

  • From clients: name, email, phone, company, and the details of the request you submit through our contact form.
  • About subjects: the identifying and record data needed to run a requested check — which may include name, date of birth, employment and education history, corporate/CAC details, government-record identifiers, and publicly available digital/media information.
  • Automatically: [STATE WHETHER YOU USE ANALYTICS/COOKIES — see section 9].

4. The lawful basis and consent

We verify records only where there is a lawful basis to do so. For subject data, we rely on consent — the subject must have agreed to being checked — and/or the legitimate interest of a client conducting lawful due diligence, as permitted under the NDPA. We do not run a check on a subject who has not consented where consent is required. Clients confirm they have obtained the necessary consent before submitting a request.

5. How we use the data

  • To carry out the specific verification or screening you request.
  • To contact the issuing institution or file the official application needed to confirm a record.
  • To prepare and deliver a confidential report to the requesting client.
  • To respond to your enquiry and manage our relationship with you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not aggregate or resell verification results beyond the client who requested them.

6. Who we share data with

To confirm a record at source, we necessarily disclose the minimum data required to the relevant institution or authority — for example an employer, an examination body, the Corporate Affairs Commission, the Nigeria Police Force, the National Population Commission, or the Federal Road Safety Corps. We do not otherwise disclose personal data to third parties except where required by law.

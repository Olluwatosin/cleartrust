Immigration Compliance Service

Document verification and compliance support for organizations and individuals navigating cross-border hiring, visas, and work authorization.

What this service is for

Organizations hiring across borders, and individuals relocating for work, both face the same underlying problem we solve elsewhere: documents need to be genuine, and paperwork needs to be complete and correct before it reaches an immigration authority. This service applies the same primary-source discipline to immigration documentation.

Typical scope

  • Verifying the authenticity of supporting documents (educational credentials, employment letters, identity documents) submitted as part of a visa or work permit application.
  • Reviewing an employer's documentation for compliance with work-permit sponsorship requirements before submission.
  • Advisory support on documentation gaps that commonly cause delays or rejections.

Early-stage note

This is a newer service line for us, and scope varies by case — country of destination, visa category, and employer requirements all change what's actually needed. Rather than list a fixed package here, we'd rather scope it properly with you: tell us the specific situation (which country, which visa category, employer or individual applicant) and we'll tell you plainly what we can and can't help with before any engagement starts.

What Drives Us

Mission & vision

Africa has a verification gap — forged documents, unverifiable claims, and guesswork stand in for real due diligence. We built ClearTrust to close it, one confirmed record at a time.

Our Mission

To close Africa's verification gap by replacing guesswork and unverifiable claims with direct confirmation from the source — giving employers, institutions, and individuals a single point of truth they can act on with confidence.

Our Vision

A pan-African standard for trust, where a credential, a record, or a reputation can be verified with the same rigor anywhere on the continent — and where "verified by ClearTrust" means exactly what it says.

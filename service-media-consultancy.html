Services/Media Consultancy

Media Consultancy

Advisory support for organizations managing public reputation, media exposure, and communications risk.

What this service is for

Verification and reputation are two sides of the same coin — one confirms facts about a person or entity, the other manages how those facts (and public narrative more broadly) are presented and defended. This service line exists for organizations that need help on the second half of that problem.

Typical scope

  • Media monitoring — tracking how an organization or individual is being discussed in the press and online.
  • Reputation risk assessment — identifying exposure before it becomes a crisis.
  • Communications advisory — guidance on messaging and response during a sensitive or high-visibility situation.

Early-stage note

This is a newer, more advisory-driven service line than our verification work, and engagements are scoped individually rather than sold as a fixed package. If you're considering this service, the most useful next step is a conversation about your specific situation — what's at stake, what's already happened, and what outcome you're trying to reach — before we describe exactly what we'd do.

Delivery Method

Case-by-case scoping
Media monitoring & assessment
Communications advisory
